# Bike Buyers Factors Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI Dashboard** that analyzes the key factors influencing bike purchase decisions.  
This project helps identify patterns related to demographics, income, education, occupation, family size, home ownership, and commute distance among bike buyers.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of **1,000 bike buyers** and visualizes the relationship between various customer attributes and their likelihood of purchasing a bike.

### Key Visuals

| Visual                      | Description                                              |
|----------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| **Percentage of Buyers**   | Donut chart showing overall Yes vs No purchase ratio    |
| **Buyers by Education**    | Comparison of purchase behaviour across education levels |
| **Age Wise Buyers**        | Horizontal bar chart by Age Group (Adolescent, Middle Age, Old) |
| **Number of Cars**         | Purchase behaviour based on number of cars owned        |
| **Gender**                 | Male vs Female distribution of buyers                   |
| **Income wise Behaviour**  | Income range impact on bike purchase                    |
| **Home Ownership**         | Effect of owning a home on bike buying decision         |
| **Family Size**            | Influence of family size on purchase                    |
| **Marital Status**         | Married vs Single buyers distribution                   |
| **Buyers by Occupation**   | Purchase trends across different occupations            |
| **Commute Distance**       | Relationship between commute distance and bike purchase |

## 🔑 Key Insights from the Dashboard

- **Overall Purchase Rate**: Approximately **51.9%** of customers purchased a bike, while **48.1%** did not.
- **Education**: Higher education levels (Graduate Degree, Bachelor) show stronger purchase intent.
- **Age**: Middle-aged customers form the largest group of bike buyers.
- **Gender**: Nearly balanced distribution between Male and Female buyers.
- **Income**: Purchase behaviour peaks in the mid-income ranges.
- **Home Ownership**: Home owners show different purchase patterns compared to non-owners.
- **Commute Distance**: Customers with shorter commute distances (0-1 Miles and 1-2 Miles) have higher purchase rates.
- **Occupation**: Professional and Skilled Manual occupations show higher bike buying tendency.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download the `.pbix` file from this repository.
2. Open it using **Power BI Desktop**.
3. Explore the interactive visuals:
   - Hover over charts for detailed tooltips
   - Use filters (if available) to drill down into specific segments
4. Analyze how different factors (Age, Income, Education, Occupation, etc.) influence bike purchase decisions.
